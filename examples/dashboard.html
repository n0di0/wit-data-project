Netflix Catalog Dashboard

Four charts about what is in Netflix's catalog: what kinds of titles, from where, and how old.

1. When were these titles released?

Question: is the catalog mostly new? 68% of the sample was released in 2015 or later.

2. Which genres show up most?

Question: what is Netflix known for? Titles can have several genres, so each one counts in every genre it lists.

3. How is the catalog rated?

Question: who is it made for? Movies are 67% of this sample, and the ratings lean toward mature audiences (TV-MA, TV-14, and R lead).

4. Which countries make the most titles?

Question: how global is it? Co-productions count once for each country listed.
