Does Netflix Add New Titles or Old Ones?

A short case study of how old a title is when it arrives on Netflix.

Question

When Netflix adds a title, is it usually a fresh release, or something that has been around for years? And has that changed over time?

Data

Data: Netflix Movies and TV Shows (Kaggle), a catalog snapshot through 2021. This page uses a random sample of 450 titles. Check the license on the Kaggle page and credit the original author.

Age at arrival means the year a title was added minus the year it was released.

What I found

Most titles arrive within a couple of years of release. 54% of titles were added in the same calendar year as their release or the year after, and the median title was 1 year old on arrival. TV shows (69%) lean newer than movies (47%).

The age at arrival changes from year to year. Years with fewer than 10 sampled titles are left out, so the line only covers years with enough data to be meaningful.

2020 had the most additions in this sample. The dataset is a snapshot from 2021, so the last year on the chart is likely incomplete.
